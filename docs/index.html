FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this really free? What's the catch?
+
No catch. NeuralBox is open source software that runs AI models (like Qwen 3.5) directly on your PC using Ollama. There's nothing to pay for because nothing runs on our servers. You download it, install it, and it runs forever for free on your machine.
What PC specs do I need?
+
Windows 10/11 with at least 12GB RAM and 25GB free disk space. An NVIDIA GPU speeds things up significantly, but it runs on CPU too (slower). The installer checks your specs and warns you if anything is below minimum.
How is this different from ChatGPT?
+
ChatGPT is a generic chat interface — you have to engineer every prompt yourself, your data goes to OpenAI's servers, and you pay $20/month. NeuralBox has purpose-built income tools with structured forms, runs entirely on your PC (zero cloud, zero data sharing), and costs nothing. It also has 5 background AI workers that batch-generate content while you sleep.
Can I use it without internet?
+
Yes. Once installed (which requires internet to download the ~5GB AI model), NeuralBox runs 100% offline. No internet required to generate content. Your data never leaves your machine.
What about the cloud API option?
+
Optionally, you can connect your own Groq (free tier available), OpenAI, or Anthropic API keys for higher quality output on specific tasks. You pay those providers directly — NeuralBox takes zero cut. There's a built-in daily spending cap to prevent surprises.
Is the quality good enough to actually use?
+
The default model (Qwen 3.5 9B) produces results on par with GPT-3.5 for content creation tasks. TikTok hooks, email copy, and short-form scripts are where it excels. For longer, more complex work like full proposals, activating the cloud boost with Groq's free tier brings it to GPT-4 level — still free.
